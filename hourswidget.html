Desoto Campus Library

Library facilities include study carrels, quiet group study space, and computers with Internet and Microsoft Office programs.

Collections include:

  • Reference books
  • Circulating books
  • Journals and magazines
  • Limited videotape and DVD collections

Services include:

  • Book circulation from DeSoto Campus collection
  • Book delivery from Highlands Campus Library to DeSoto Campus for student pickup
  • Printing and delivery of journal articles (when unavailable in Library databases) to site for student pickup
  • Interlibrary loan from other institutions
  • Librarian consultation at site on a rotation basis and by arrangement
  • Phone consultation with Highlands Campus Library reference staff during Highlands Campus Library hours of operation
  • Call 863-784-7304 for phone assistance by reference staff
