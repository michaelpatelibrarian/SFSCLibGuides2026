Starting Monday, Apr. 13, students may apply for or renew loaner laptops from the SFSC Library for the summer term. When they apply to renew, students must be registered for at least one college credit course in the summer term.

You must use the Laptop Appointment Request Form to schedule a time to renew so that registration can be verified beforehand.

If there are any questions or concerns, contact Lena Phelps at 863-784-7303 or via email at phelpsl@southflorida.edu or Michael Pate at 863-784-7305 or via email at patem@southflorida.edu.
